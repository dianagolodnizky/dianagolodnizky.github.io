PROBLEM STATEMENT

Individuals living with PTSD often find themselves trapped between a fragmented healthcare system-marked by long wait times and high costs- and a pervasive social stigma that fosters isolation and a fear of judgment. This invisible condition frequently disrupts daily functioning and interpersonal relationships, leaving many to navigate a loss of control and a lack of self-recognition without the support of traditional, time-bound therapy.

ABOUT

Ken Lanefesh is a deeply personalized sanctuary designed to help users build stable routines and manage recovery at their own pace. The platform integrates an anonymous community, professional resources, and a dedicated emergency zone with progress tracking and a points-based reward system. Guided by an AI bird companion for continuous support, the application provides a secure environment that bridges the gap between daily struggles and professional care through direct access to mental health experts and resilience centres.

DESIGN
CONCEPT

THE NEST METAPHOR

The visual language of the application is built around the metaphor of a bird’s nest- a secure and nurturing sanctuary. This space is designed to foster personal growth, providing users with the tools and resilience they need to eventually navigate life with confidence outside the nest.

VISUALIZING THE INVISIBLE

A core element of the design strategy is the use of transparency to represent those living with post-traumatic stress. This choice serves as a visual reflection of the lack of social recognition and understanding that often accompanies the condition. Because post-trauma is a transparent illness with no external physical markers, it is frequently overlooked or underestimated by society. The UI acknowledges this internal reality, validating the user’s experience through a sophisticated and empathetic visual language.

PRIVACY AND
PSYCHOLOGICAL SAFETY

The application places a high priority on user anonymity. Understanding that many survivors prefer discretion and may feel uncomfortable with public exposure, the platform is designed to be a private haven. By removing the pressure of identification, the interface creates a low-friction environment where users can engage, share, and seek help without the fear of being judged or exposed.

QUANTITAVE RESEARCH

To design an impactful and empathetic experience, I conducted extensive research into the mental health landscape and the specific challenges of PTSD recovery. The following data, gathered from clinical studies and market research, provides a detailed look at how accessibility, anonymity, and structured support influence the healing journey and user well-being.

3.9%

of the global population has experienced PTSD at some point in their lives, making it a widespread mental health challenge.

50%

of people living with PTSD do not seek professional treatment due to social stigma or lack of access to care.

70%

of adults worldwide experience at least one traumatic event in their lifetime, increasing the potential risk for post-traumatic symptoms.

30%

reduction in PTSD symptoms can be achieved through consistent use of digital mental health tools and self-management apps.

80%

of PTSD patients suffer from at least one co-occurring mental health condition, such as depression or anxiety, requiring a holistic support system for them..

1 in 10

women will develop PTSD at some point in their lives, which is twice the rate compared to men.

46%

of people with PTSD report significant difficulties in maintaining steady employment and stable interpersonal relationships.

55%

of PTSD survivors experience significant sleep disturbances and nightmares, which directly impacts their ability to maintain a stable daily routine.

USER NEEDS

1. IMMEDIATE ACCESS TO SUPPORT

Users need a 24/7 digital resource that provides grounding tools and professional help at any moment, especially during unpredictable periods of emotional distress.

2. A SAFE, ANONYMOUS SPACE

Survivors need a non-judgmental environment where they can share their experiences and connect with others without revealing their identity or facing social stigma.

3. ROUTINE AND STRUCTURE

Users need intuitive tools to help them organize their day and build stable habits, helping them regain a sense of control over a life that often feels chaotic.

4. HYPER-PERSONALIZATION

Individuals need a system that recognizes their unique emotional state and adapts the content and tasks to fit their specific energy levels and triggers in real-time.

5. VALIDATION AND UNDERSTANDING

Users need access to reliable information and a supportive community to help them realize they are not alone and that their symptoms are a valid response to trauma.

6. LOW-FRICTION INTERACTION

Because PTSD can cause cognitive overload, users need a simple, calming interface that is easy to navigate even during moments of high anxiety.

FEATURES &
FUNCTIONALITIES

TO RESOLVE USER NEEDS

A tailored questionnaire and profile setup that adapt the app's content and tools to each user’s specific emotional state and privacy preferences.

Real time content adaptation based on daily mood selection and sensor data to detect emotional shifts and offer proactive support.

A dynamic schedule that suggests personalized tasks, breathing exercises, and routines based on the user's current mood and the time of day.

A safe, social style environment for sharing experiences, participating in daily challenges, and seeking peer support without revealing personal identities.

An AI bird companion that provides 24/7 emotional support and guidance through a friendly, interactive chat interface.

An immediate emergency zone offering breathing exercises, direct access to professionals or volunteers, and instant links to resilience centers for urgent support.

A curated library of articles, podcasts, and expert resources to provide deep insights into PTSD and professional care options.

A visual goal-setting system that rewards daily achievements with points and incentives to maintain long-term motivation and consistency.

CHALLENGES

During moments of high anxiety, users find it difficult to navigate complex interfaces or process large amounts of information.

LACK OF ROUTINE

FEAR OF TRIGGERING

COMPETITOR
ANALYSIS

PTSD GUIDE ISRAEL (MINISTRY OF DEFENSE)

Originally developed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) for American veterans, this app was translated into Hebrew and adapted for the Israeli public by the Ministry of Defense's Rehabilitation Department.

FEATURES

The app offers professional information on post-trauma, self-calming tools, coping strategies, personal tracking options, and recommendations for further professional treatment. Use of the app is anonymous and free of charge.

PTSD COACH

This is the original American version of the PTSD Guide, developed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs to assist veterans and civilians globally.

FEATURES

The app provides information on post-trauma, symptom management tools, self-assessment, and additional support resources. It is available for free and is used by many people worldwide.

OCD.APP

This app utilizes AI-driven cognitive behavioral techniques to help users manage obsessive thoughts and anxiety, making it a powerful tool for trauma-related distress.

FEATURES

The app offers short daily CBT micro-training sessions, an AI-powered thought analysis tool for reframing negative patterns, and gamified progress tracking to ensure long-term consistency.

HEADSPACE

This app uses a science-backed approach to mindfulness with structured courses designed to reduce stress and improve mental resilience through friendly, guided animations.

FEATURES

The app offers themed meditation courses for anxiety and trauma, "Sleepcasts" that create a calm environment for rest, SOS sessions for immediate panic relief, and an empathetic AI companion named Ebb for personalized mental health recommendations.

UNIQUE
FEATURES

TAILORED ONBOARDING & ANONYMOUS PROFILING

A personalized entry process featuring a brief assessment to accurately map the user's mental state, ensuring all content and tools are precisely calibrated to their specific needs while maintaining 100% anonymity.

DUAL-LAYER MOOD ADAPTATION

A dynamic system that suggests personalized tasks and routines based on the user's active daily check-in ("How are you feeling today?"), combined with passive analysis from phone and smartwatch sensors to detect subconscious emotional shifts.

ADAPTIVE DAILY ROUTINE MANAGEMENT

An intelligent engine that organizes the user's day by offering filtered tasks- such as movement, breathing exercises, and essential reading- optimized according to their current mood and time of day.

SAFE-SPACE COMMUNITY NETWORK

An anonymous, sensitive social environment where users can share experiences, participate in daily challenges, and seek immediate community support during moments of emotional distress.

INTEGRATED AI BIRD COMPANION

A virtual guide that provides constant presence and AI-powered conversational support, offering guidance and a listening ear whenever the user needs it.

MULTILAYERED EMERGENCY ZONE

A dedicated space for acute distress that provides immediate grounding tools, direct access to pre-selected personal contacts, and instant links to professional resilience centers and volunteers.

GAMIFIED RECOVERY JOURNEY

A motivational system where users set personal goals, track their long-term progress, and earn points for completing daily tasks that can be redeemed for rewards and benefits.

PROFESSIONAL RESOURCE LIBRARY

A comprehensive hub offering in-depth knowledge through articles and podcasts, alongside a direct directory to professional therapists and localized support centers.

USER PERSONA 1
NAME:

Shahar

AGE:

30

LOCATION:

Northern Israel

MARITAL STATUS:

Single father (1 child)

OCCUPATION

Renovation worker

USAGE SCENARIO

Shahar is a combat veteran dealing with severe PTSD. His work in renovations often triggers flashbacks of urban warfare. He finds traditional therapy overwhelming and inconsistent, leading him to seek a discreet, digital alternative. He uses the app during high-stress moments at work or at night when he struggles to remain calm and present for his 6-year-old daughter.

MOTIVATIONS AND GOALS
  • Wants accessible tools to manage daily stress and maintain control, especially when parenting.
  • Aims to build a stable framework that helps him function better in his relationships and career.
  • Seeks a non-judgmental, private space to process emotions without the intensity of face-to-face therapy.
PREFERNCES AND BEHAVIOR
  • Needs a simple and fast interface that works during crises or late-night episodes.
  • Prefers content that adapts to his immediate mood- practical tools for "lows" and empowering content for "highs."
  • Values a quiet support system that doesn't overwhelm him with daily notifications and guarantees total anonymity.
PAIN POINTS
  • Struggles with lack of sleep, which severely impacts his daytime productivity and mood.
  • Sudden noises and specific environments trigger flashbacks and emotional dysregulation.
  • Difficulty maintaining family and romantic connections, leading to deep loneliness and self-doubt.

OCCUPATION

USAGE SCENARIO

Michal struggles with complex post trauma following past sexual trauma. She often feels paralyzed by exhaustion especially during mornings and nights. While in therapy she finds face to face sessions draining and often disconnects when overwhelmed. She views the app as a silent companion that helps her survive another day without the pressure of explaining her pain to others.

MOTIVATIONS AND GOALS
  • Needs a safe space that functions when she has no words or strength to speak.
  • Seeks simple tools to lower emotional overload and navigate through unbearable moments.
  • Aims to better recognize her emotional state and access immediate relief tools without effort.
PREFERNCES AND BEHAVIOR
  • Prefers a soothing design with soft colors and gentle motion to avoid sensory overwhelm.
  • Values a no judgment zone where she can choose her pace without being pressured by daily streaks or tasks.
  • Uses the app for rapid grounding when on the verge of a breakdown or during sudden flashbacks.
PAIN POINTS
  • Severe difficulty functioning during transition periods like mornings and late nights due to heavy emotional fatigue.
  • Finds it hard to maintain regular treatment often quitting when the emotional exposure feels too intense.
  • Struggles with lingering feelings of self blame that prevent her from reaching out for traditional help.

BRAND GUIDELINES

As part of the project, I developed a comprehensive brand book that defines the visual and conceptual identity of Ken Lenefesh. The book covers the entire process from research to final execution. While the brand book was physically printed, an interactive version is also available for exploration within the project file.

The brand book begins with a project introduction and a dedication to those coping with PTSD to establish a supportive foundation, followed by an in-depth research section that explores the impact of trauma, competitive analysis, and identified pain points. It then outlines the project’s vision and core values that guided the design, along with a detailed breakdown of the visual language, typography, color palette, logo design, custom icons, and print materials. Finally, the book presents the primary outcomes- the mobile application and the promotional website.
